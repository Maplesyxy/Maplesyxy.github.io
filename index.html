About

I’m a first-year at Yale studying Math & Computer Science and Economics, and a Hillhouse Fellow. I like creating new things and am almost always working on a project.

My research so far has lived at the intersection of deep learning and the sciences: a custom Vision Transformer for sparse event-camera data, a GVP-GCN + Transformer for RNA inverse folding that beat RDesign, RiboLogic and LEARNA on RNA-Puzzles, and an interpretable Grad-CAM ViT for early Alzheimer’s diagnosis from MRI. Most recently I’ve applied the same toolkit to financial signal prediction as a quant research intern.

Before Yale I graduated cum laude from The Lawrenceville School as a Hutchins Jefferson Science Scholar, and was named a 2026 U.S. Presidential Scholar.
